Lusaka, Zambia AgriFuel Green Energy Limited is proud to announce its selection as a Social Impact Award (SIA) 2026 Winner (East & Southern Africa). Competing among hundreds of innovative changemakers across the region, AgriFuel Green Energy stood out for its pioneering approach to converting agricultural crop waste into clean-burning, affordable eco-briquettes, directly addressing rural energy poverty and deforestation.

### Scaling Sustainable Solutions in Zambia

Founded by cybersecurity professional and social entrepreneur Astone Mwewa Mwamba (brand name Mwewa Mwamba), AgriFuel Green Energy operates out of the Lusaka South Multi-Facility Economic Zone (LS-MFEZ). Our eco-briquettes provide a reliable, smokeless alternative to traditional charcoal, delivering a consistent 4 to 5-hour burn duration for households while partnering with smallholder farmers to recover crop residues that would otherwise be openly burned.

This recognition underscores our commitment to localized climate action, sustainable industrialization, and green economic empowerment. Winning the SIA 2026 award validates our pilot trials in communities like Mulakupikwa Village and Chinsali, and fuels our momentum as we scale our manufacturing capacity and raw material aggregation hubs.

### Representing Zambia on the Global Stage

As part of this milestone achievement, our team is preparing to travel to Podgorica, Montenegro, in November 2026 to represent Zambia at the prestigious Social Impact Award International Summit. This global platform will allow us to connect with international investors, climate tech leaders, and fellow innovators shaping the future of sustainable enterprise.

### Looking Ahead

This award is not just a win for AgriFuel Green Energy—it is a testament to the power of youth-led innovation in driving Africa’s green transition. We extend our sincere gratitude to the Social Impact Award organizers, our regional mentors, our dedicated team, and every community member who supported our vision.

As we expand our impact, we invite partners, investors, and stakeholders to join us in scaling clean, accessible energy across Zambia and beyond. Together, we are turning agricultural waste into a catalyst for sustainable development.\
